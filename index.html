This is what you need.

Multitrack & routing

Balance backing tracks, click and cues. Shape each track with four-band EQ, then route through buses to stereo or separate USB outputs. Native track EQ requires V27.26 or newer; master compression is available in Browser.

Lyrics & chords

Choose lyrics, chords or both, follow autoscroll and keep the current section and the next one in view.

Rehearsal tools

Start from a song region, add a count-in, adjust tempo and transpose selected tracks to rehearse in another key.

Host & Follow Lyrics

Host the lyric display and let other devices follow the current song and lyric position over your local network.

Personalization

Choose a skin, coordinated colours and controls at the bottom or either side. Each device can keep its own layout.

Setlists & documents

Organise your songs into sets, create song and setlist PDFs, and keep your profile and document details together.

Features

Audio & mixer

  • Native multitrack playback
  • Track levels, pan, mute and solo
  • Four-band EQ per track in Native V27.26+ and Browser
  • Optional master compressor in Browser
  • Stereo practice and separate USB outputs
  • Output buses and mute groups
  • Song Loudness with a target LUFS level

Lyrics & display

  • Lyrics, chords or both
  • Autoscroll and song sections
  • Capo & Key with optional player names
  • Chord diagrams and region colours
  • Full screen and adjustable live controls

Rehearsal

  • Tempo and selected-track transpose
  • Song and region count-ins
  • Metronome sound, level and output
  • Optional visual beat flash
  • Built-in tuner

Connections

  • REAPER song and region import
  • MIDI pedals for hands-free control
  • MIDI Clock with optional Start/Stop
  • Cues & Show for Soundcraft Ui24R
  • Host & Follow Lyrics over a local network

Library & sharing

  • Song library and setlists
  • Concert transfer between your devices
  • Cloud-backed song and set sharing
  • Full backup with audio
  • Review possible duplicate songs

Band & planning

  • Rehearsals and concert dates
  • Venue contacts and stage notes
  • Setlists and editable packing lists
  • Band invitations and attendance replies
  • Administrator-only contracts and fees

Website privacy

The optional Song editor uses Supabase for email sign-in and a private online library of song titles, artists, keys and lyrics with chords/sections. These are sent only when you choose to save or sync. Audio is not uploaded by the editor. Drafts are stored in this browser; sign-in sessions are stored for the browser tab. Signing out does not erase your drafts. Use your own computer for private songs.

This website has no advertising trackers, analytics or contact forms. Search and filters run in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information.

YouTube videos are not embedded or loaded automatically. Video links open YouTube only after you choose one.

The app’s privacy information will be available before its App Store release.
