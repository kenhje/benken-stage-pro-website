01 / INVITE

Your band, together.

Create a band and share a private invitation link. Members sign in with their own email and join your band.

02 / PREPARE

Everything for the date.

Publish a rehearsal or concert with its venue, arrival and soundcheck times, setlists, song lyrics and equipment list.

03 / REPLY

Know who can make it.

Members reply going, cannot come or unsure. When the event changes, they confirm again. Check equipment as packed and brought home.

Contracts stay with the administrator.

Fees and contracts are stored separately with administrator-only access. Members see the practical information they need.

Plan sharing and audio sharing are separate.

Event setlists contain a snapshot of song information and lyrics. Use song/setlist sharing to send audio. Export a calendar copy for Apple or Google Calendar.

Development version. Band planning needs a configured cloud service and email sign-in. Open the band page and refresh for updates. Automatic email/push alerts and two-way calendar synchronisation are not included in this first version.

LIVE / HOST & FOLLOW

Your lyrics, on their screens.

Start lyric sharing on the host. Bandmates join through the shared link or QR code on the same local network. Keep the host open while you play, and let followers stay with the current song and lyric position.

This shares the lyric display. It does not install backing tracks or synchronise audio playback between devices.

BEFORE THE SHOW / SHARE A SET

Send the music ahead.

Share one song or a complete setlist with audio through a cloud-backed link or QR code. Choose an expiry for the link. The recipient downloads the shared content to prepare and rehearse on their own device.

Cloud sharing needs a configured storage service and an internet connection. It is separate from live lyric following and from a full backup.

ON STAGE / MIDI

Keep your hands on the music.

Map a compatible MIDI foot controller to actions such as Play/Pause, Stop, Next and mute groups. Send MIDI Clock to a selected MIDI output so compatible loopers and other equipment can follow the song’s tempo.

MIDI Start/Stop is optional. Tempo clock does not automatically align a looper’s song position after seeking. Test your pedal, interface and clock setup before a show.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before the first song.

A few useful answers about BenKen Stage Pro.

Does Ask BenKen need AI or an internet connection?

No. It searches the manual included with the app, using feature names, keywords and common questions. Results show the manual instructions with shortcuts to the relevant settings. It does not generate answers, upload questions or change settings for you.

What is the difference between Follow Lyrics and sharing a set?

Follow Lyrics is a live display connection: a host leads and other devices follow the text. Sharing a set sends downloadable song content, including audio when included, through cloud storage. Concert sync transfers content between your own devices. These are separate workflows.

Can I use BenKen offline?

Playback uses locally stored audio. Make sure your songs, audio and selected concert are downloaded before going offline. Cloud sharing and other online features require a connection.

Which audio files can I use?

BenKen supports MP3, WAV and M4A. REAPER import brings backing tracks and region information into the app workflow.

Can I send tracks to separate outputs?

Yes, with a compatible USB audio interface or mixer that provides enough outputs. Stereo combines tracks into one stereo mix, while USB routing lets you use separate outputs. Test your routing with your own hardware.

Do all devices follow playback automatically?

Concert sync transfers content and audio between devices. It does not provide simultaneous playback start, continuous playback sync or automatic failover. Lyrics sharing can let other devices follow the lyric display.

When will the app be on the App Store?

BenKen Stage Pro is in development and testing. A release date, pricing and any subscription plans have not been set. This page presents the development version; the final App Store release may differ.
